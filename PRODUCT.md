# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Academy owners managing collections and schedules; coaches recording attendance
on phones outdoors; receptionists using shared front-desk devices; platform
operators administering tenants; students and guardians receiving updates.

## Product Purpose

Aqua runs sports academies and recreation facilities in India, replacing
disconnected registers, spreadsheets and messages with scoped operational
workflows. Source: `docs/project-scope.md` and `docs/architecture.md`.

## Brand Personality

Practical, legible, precise. Follow the existing `DESIGN.md` identity.

## Anti-references

Decorative status colors, generic metric-card dashboards, fabricated delivery
states and nested cards. Existing reference boards and `DESIGN.md` govern
composition; do not invent a new visual identity for workflow additions.

## Design Principles

- Show operational state and the next permitted action clearly.
- Keep role-specific workflows usable on their documented viewports.
- Preserve tenant boundaries, exact money and explicit permission checks.
- Distinguish persisted success, pending delivery and retryable failure.
- Keep parent pages lightweight and zero-JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use existing tokens, normal-text contrast of at least 4.5:1, visible focus,
44px touch targets, 16px input text, reduced-motion support and readable
states in outdoor light. Sources: `DESIGN.md` and scope §6.4.
