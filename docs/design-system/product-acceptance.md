# Product acceptance and task contracts

Human-authorised direction, 31 October 2026. This reconciles older composition,
visible-copy and universal icon/chip prescriptions. It is an acceptance contract,
not evidence that existing screens pass. Security, permissions, financial
definitions, consent, offline recovery, parent zero-JS and budgets still apply.

## One product, task-specific composition

Cover Owner/Admin, Ops, coach, reception, worker/staff, accountant/finance,
parent/member-facing and public/auth/onboarding/shared-link consumers. Map actual
routes and capabilities; mark planned or unsupported work explicitly. Do not
invent features to complete a grid. Every future screen adds a coverage entry.

Mint Garden and Lavender Club share component behaviour and semantic meanings.
Phone and desktop may use different ordering, grids, density, disclosures and
icon sizes. Ops remains desktop-first. Preserve drafts, selected records,
filters, focus and unresolved transactions through layout changes.

## Meaning and copy ownership

- Name the user's question before composing the section. Money contains
  collections, balances and payment mix. Attendance has its own operational
  context; an attendance switch inside a Money section is rejected.
- Assign each fact, period/facility label and comparison one display owner.
  Adjacent components must not independently repeat the same scope or sentence.
  Repetition at a consequential confirmation is justified separately.
- Show concise values, units, scope and actions by default. Exact comparison
  instants, calculation definitions and less-used methodology belong in an
  accessible Details disclosure or the appropriate report. Keep uncertainty,
  consent, safety and transaction/recovery outcomes visible where needed.
- Distinguish overlapping summaries from identical data: selected-period and
  last-30-days attendance cannot be merged without preserving their meanings.
  Missing marks remain unknown. A payment QR/request is never confirmation.
- Use truthful terms: recorded payments; membership plan where that is the
  actual entity; parent-link content described by its real visibility; an
  empty Ops attention queue does not establish universal tenant health.

## Compact charts and purposeful geometry

Dashboard trends default to Last 7 days: today and the preceding six IST dates,
seven chronological daily buckets, today partial. Provide a compact range
toggle for shorter/longer views. Monday-to-now is not Last 7 days. Do not hide
monthly ticks and call it a seven-day chart. Related financial panels and
drill-downs share the selected scope. Compare equivalent elapsed ranges.
Scheduling calendars and detailed-report defaults are separate contracts.

Keep axes/units and accessible exact data. Use content-appropriate chart frames;
align designated headers/plot regions for related desktop panels. Avoid oversized
empty cards, but do not impose identical heights on unrelated content. Exercise
range changes, disclosures, empty/unknown/error, long labels and resizing.

Icons use sizes suited to navigation, inline status, table actions and shortcuts.
Compact redundant padding and gaps while keeping readable text, visible focus
and usable targets. Glyph size is distinct from the hit area. Label unfamiliar
symbols. Custom icons require asset and rendered review in both themes; duotone
details cannot be converted to one colour without checking their geometry.
Do not tint every micro-control or reuse semantic alarm/success colours merely
to decorate categories. Existing shared controls own picker/button geometry.

## Bounded task packet

Record before implementation:

1. Goal, actor/tenant/location/grants, existing versus planned capability.
2. Baseline/worktree, exclusive files, shared owner and dependencies.
3. Information owners, default/detail copy, phone/desktop compositions, scroll
   owners and required populated/empty/loading/error/denied/offline states.
4. Intended RED assertion and command; relevant positive and negative cases.
5. Functional, semantic and visual criteria; sensitive-state and budget rules.
6. Exact approved provider/model and observed session identity; stop on quota,
   routing mismatch or unapproved fallback. Never include credentials.
7. Heavy-job queue, bounded next step and durable handoff/evidence paths.

## Independent finish gate

The builder supplies actual RED, GREEN and refactor evidence, changed files and
unrun gates. A reviewer then completes entry → decision → action → result →
recovery on the actual local artifact. Review each affected shared consumer;
do not certify the whole app from one Home screenshot or a contact sheet.

Record separate functional, semantic and visual verdicts with source/diff/build
identity, fixtures, role, URL, viewport and state. Hashes establish provenance,
not truth. Screenshot counts, source classes and a green suite cannot establish
information hierarchy, recognisability or real interaction. Missing evidence is
BLOCKED; visible defects are CHANGES REQUIRED. Human demo/design approval stays
separate from checks. No local pass authorises deployment.

The coverage ledger records PASS, CHANGES REQUIRED, BLOCKED, NOT TESTED,
PLANNED or NOT APPLICABLE per role/workflow/device/state. Carry unresolved items
forward explicitly. Measure rework, escaped defects, review effort and total
wall time including queue waits; do not claim productivity from agent count.
