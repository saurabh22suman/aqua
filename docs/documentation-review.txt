## 1. Understanding
=====================================================================

Aqua is a multi-tenant SaaS operating system for sports academies and
recreation facilities in India, launched vertically into swimming clubs
(reference tenant: Aqua Club, pool + café). Tenants configure the product
through feature entitlements and presets rather than code forks — one
deployment, shared Postgres schema, RLS-enforced isolation.

The lead differentiator is batch profitability (revenue net of coach cost),
which no competitor surfaces; secondary wedges are zero commission on
payments and role-shaped interfaces (owner/coach/receptionist/worker/parent
each get distinct surfaces; parents get WhatsApp magic links, never
accounts).

Competitive reality: collections/UPI auto-debit was the original wedge but
GymForce already claims it in gyms; pricing must sit under ₹1,000–3,500/month
against Sportia's ₹999 flat, so economics demand boring infrastructure
(monolith, single DB, pg-boss) and metered WhatsApp costs.

Delivery is AI-agent-driven against a 137-task plan (Phases 0–3, go-live at
end of Phase 3), with tiered testing where humans own Tier 1 tests because
agents cannot be trusted to test their own misunderstandings.

Open strategic wound: distribution has no owner and no plan.

## 3. Unstated assumptions
=====================================================================

Numbers and rules presented without source or derivation:

- COACH PAY: "A 4-student batch with a coach on ₹800 a session loses ₹5,600
  a month" (project-scope.md §1). Neither rate nor loss is derived; the real
  figures are explicitly unknown until Phase 0 (D-03, D-06).

- WHATSAPP COSTS: "~1,800 msgs @ ~₹0.15 incl. BSP markup" and 18% GST (§2.5
  unit economics); "Marketing templates cost roughly seven to eight times
  what utility templates cost" (§2.5, architecture.md §11.2, standing
  rules). No BSP chosen (open question #4); pricing is a Meta/BSP claim
  nobody has verified.

- UNIT-ECONOMICS FILLERS: infrastructure share −₹150/month, support
  "amortised, ~20 min/month" −₹250 (§2.5). Both invented; the 75%
  contribution figure and the §12 ">70% gross margin" success metric
  inherit them.

- PRICING OPTIONS THEMSELVES: ₹1,499 / ₹999 / ₹2,499 / four-band ladder
  (§2.5) — no willingness-to-pay or cost basis given; the ₹1,000–3,500
  "ceiling" rests on vendor marketing the doc itself discredits (§2.2
  research note).

- MARKET DATA: 226 academy companies, $1.37M decade funding, $254M market @
  6.85% CAGR (§2.3) — attributed to Tracxn but unverifiable in-repo; PayLap
  "₹3,499 for twelve months" and Akton "₹89 per unit" (§2.2) use unexplained
  units.

- RBI MANDATE BAND: "recurring auto-debits up to ₹15,000 per transaction
  clear without additional authentication" (§7.3, architecture.md §10.4)
  asserted confidently while the adjacent notification window is flagged
  "24 versus 72 — verify". Asymmetric confidence in two halves of the same
  regulation.

- TAX DEFAULT: tax_rate_bp int not null default 1800 (architecture.md §8.6)
  hard-codes an assumption that everything is taxable at 18%; no SAC/HSN
  mapping exists anywhere.

- DUNNING INTERVALS 3/7/14/30 days (architecture.md §9, V-14) and config
  defaults late_threshold_minutes: 10, max_days_ahead: 30,
  cancellation_window_hours: 4 (§7.2) — plausible, entirely arbitrary.

- SCALE PROJECTIONS: 25–50 tenants, ~50k people, 500k sessions/year, 6M
  attendance rows/year, <200 concurrent, <50 writes/s (§2) — modelled from
  nothing observable.

- ENGINEERING THRESHOLDS: partition at ~10M rows, pg-boss to ~1,000
  jobs/min, page at >1% error rate / >80% DB connections (§8.5, §3.1, §14).

- UX BENCHMARKS USED AS ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA: member added "<ninety seconds"
  (C-06), 16 students marked "under sixty seconds" (C-23), walk-in booked
  "under a minute" (V-04), QR check-in "under five seconds" (V-25). None
  observed; D-01 may falsify them.

- POLICY CONSTANTS WITH NO ORIGIN: OTP 6 digits/5 min/5 attempts, magic-link
  7-day expiry, brand assets 500 KB/512 px, backups 30-day retention,
  sessions generated eight weeks ahead, uptime 99.5%.

- LEAVE BALANCES: leave_types.annual_quota implies calendar-year grants, but
  accrual/carryover/proration rules are nowhere stated, yet V-26's
  acceptance is "balances decrement correctly".

=====================================================================

  plan slabs from scope §2.4": §2.4 contains no slabs (wrong
  cross-reference; slabs are §2.5, which voids them), and the pricing model

  `plan_features`, and `tenants.plan_id` all reference it.

  design decision (view, computed-at-read, or nightly update) before the

- F-03/F-04 — users, tenant_memberships, roles, permissions,

  only architecture.md §19 mentions them in passing. Session linkage between

- V-18 — Makeup credits: "an excused absence grants exactly one makeup

## 5. First three questions
=====================================================================

1. WHICH PRICING MODEL — A, B, OR C? F-01 seeds plans in week ~4 of Phase 1,
   F-13 builds entitlement resolution on it, quota limits and the §12 NRR
   metric depend on it — yet the decision is deferred to "before Phase 3".
   Everything platform-side downstream of plan_features is guesswork until
   this lands.

2. WHAT ARE AQUA CLUB'S ACTUAL COACH PAY STRUCTURES AND FEE ARRANGEMENTS?
   (Phase 0, D-03/D-06.) The lead differentiator — batch profitability — is
   a formula over data whose real shape nobody has written down; if pay is
   not actually session-derived, V-30/V-36 and the entire positioning
   statement change.

3. WHERE DO TENANTS 2–10 COME FROM? Scope §11 rates distribution High
   likelihood/High impact and admits "Nothing in this plan addresses
   distribution"; open question #1 confirms no owner. If the answer is
   "nobody knows", Phase 4's exit criteria (three to five paying tenants)
   are fiction and the build order itself is arguable.
