Why this stays light

  • No illustration. The hero wave and the ring on the parent card are inline SVG paths — about 300 bytes together, versus 200–400 KB for a 3D character render.
  • Colour carries meaning. Mango is only ever an action. Water is data. Green and red are payment and attendance state. Nothing is coloured for decoration.
  • Two fonts, subset. Bricolage Grotesque for display and figures, Instrument Sans for body — roughly 40 KB self-hosted, and it is where the personality actually comes from.
  • Depth from two shadows. One tight contact shadow, one soft ambient. Defined once as variables and never improvised per component.
  • The lane strip repeats. Owner sees capacity, coach sees progress through the register, parent sees membership runway. One shape, three meanings.
  • Touch targets are 36–44px. The coach's present and absent buttons are separate hit areas, not a swipe, because that screen gets used with wet hands in sunlight.
